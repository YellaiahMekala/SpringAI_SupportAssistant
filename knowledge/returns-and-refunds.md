# Returns and refunds

This document describes the returns and refunds policy of the demo store. All figures are fictional.

## Return window

You can return most items within 30 days of the delivery date. The 30 days are counted from the day the order is marked as delivered, not from the day it was placed. Returns requested after 30 days are not accepted, except for faulty items covered by the warranty policy.

## Items that can be returned

An item can be returned if it is unused, in its original packaging, and has all its tags and accessories. Items that were opened but are not damaged can still be returned, but a restocking fee of 10 percent may apply to electronics.

## Items that cannot be returned

Gift cards, digital downloads, perishable goods, and items made to order cannot be returned. Hygiene products such as earphones and personal care items cannot be returned once the seal is broken.

## How to start a return

Sign in, open the order, and choose "Start a return". Pick the item and the reason. You will receive a prepaid return label by email. Pack the item and hand it to the carrier within 7 days of receiving the label.

## Refund timing and method

A refund is issued after the returned item reaches our warehouse and passes inspection. Inspection takes up to 2 business days. The refund is then sent to the original payment method and usually appears within 5 to 7 business days. Refunds are never paid to a different card or account.

## Damaged or wrong items

If an item arrives damaged or you received the wrong item, contact support within 14 days of delivery. We pay the return shipping and send a replacement or a full refund, as you prefer.
