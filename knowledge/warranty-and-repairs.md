# Warranty and repairs

This document describes the warranty for products bought from the demo store. All figures are fictional.

## Warranty period

Electronics have a warranty of 24 months from the delivery date. Other products have a warranty of 12 months. The warranty covers faults in materials and workmanship.

## What is not covered

The warranty does not cover damage caused by misuse, accidents, liquid damage, or repairs made by anyone other than an approved repair partner. Normal wear, such as scratches on a case or a worn battery after heavy use, is not covered.

## Making a claim

Open the order and choose "Report a fault". Describe the problem and add photos if you can. We reply within 2 business days with the next step, which is a replacement, a repair, or a refund.

## Repairs

Repairs take up to 15 business days after the item reaches the repair partner. We provide a prepaid label. If the item cannot be repaired, we offer a replacement or a refund of the purchase price.

## Replacement items

A replacement item carries the remaining warranty of the original item, or 6 months, whichever is longer.
