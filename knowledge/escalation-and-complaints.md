# Escalation and complaints

This document describes how to escalate an issue and how long complaints take. All figures are fictional.

## When to escalate

You can ask for an issue to be escalated if you have contacted support twice about the same problem without a solution, or if 5 business days have passed since your first contact without a resolution.

## How to escalate

Reply to your support case and write "escalate" in the first line, or choose "Escalate this case" on the case page. Include the order reference if the issue is about an order. A supervisor will pick up the case.

## Response times

A supervisor responds to an escalated case within 2 business days. A formal complaint receives a written response within 10 business days. We tell you if more time is needed and why.

## Formal complaints

If you are not satisfied with the outcome of an escalated case, you can make a formal complaint. The complaint is reviewed by someone who was not involved in the original case.

## What we need from you

To help us resolve an issue quickly, include the order reference, what you expected, and what happened. Do not include passwords, one-time codes or card security codes.
