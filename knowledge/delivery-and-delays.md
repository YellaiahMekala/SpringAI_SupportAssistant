# Delivery and delays

This document describes delivery times and what happens when an order is late. All figures are fictional.

## Delivery options and times

Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 business days after the order has shipped. Express delivery takes 1 to 2 business days after shipping. Orders are usually shipped within 1 business day of being placed. Delivery on weekends and public holidays is not available.

## Tracking an order

Once an order has shipped, the order page shows its milestones: placed, shipped, and delivered. The promised date shown on the order is the latest date we expect delivery. Tracking updates can take up to 24 hours to appear after the carrier scans a package.

## When an order is delayed

An order is marked as delayed when it will not arrive by the promised date. Common reasons are carrier capacity problems, severe weather, and customs checks. We send an email when an order is marked as delayed, with a new promised date.

## What you can do about a delay

If an order arrives more than 3 business days after its promised date, you can ask to cancel the order for a full refund, or accept a delivery credit of 10 percent of the order value. You do not need to wait for the order to arrive before asking.

## Lost packages

If an order has not arrived 5 business days after its promised date, it is treated as possibly lost. We open an investigation with the carrier, which takes up to 5 business days. If the package is not found, we send a replacement or refund the order in full.

## Wrong address

If the delivery address on an order is wrong, contact support before the order ships. After it has shipped, the address cannot be changed, but we can arrange a redelivery for a fee.
