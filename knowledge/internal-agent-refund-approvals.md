# Internal: agent refund approvals

INTERNAL USE ONLY. For support agents. Figures are fictional.

## Approval limits

Support agents can approve a refund up to 100 units without a supervisor. Refunds between 100 and 500 units need supervisor approval in the case system. Refunds above 500 units need a finance review.

## Goodwill credits

Agents can add a goodwill credit of up to 10 units to an account once per 90 days when an order was delayed by more than 3 business days. Credits above this limit need a supervisor.

## Fraud signals

Escalate to the fraud team, and do not issue a refund, when the delivery address was changed after the order shipped, when three or more orders to different addresses were placed within one hour, or when a customer asks for a refund to a different payment method.

## Customer identity

Verify identity by asking the customer to confirm the order reference and the delivery postcode. Never ask for a password, a one-time code or a card security code, and never read these back if a customer volunteers them.
