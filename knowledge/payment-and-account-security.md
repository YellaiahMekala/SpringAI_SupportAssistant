# Payment and account security

This document explains how payments work and what support will never ask you for. All figures are fictional.

## What support will never ask for

Support staff and this assistant will never ask for your password, one-time codes, card security codes (CVV), or full card numbers. If anyone asks you for these, do not share them, and report it to support. If you type one of these by mistake, the assistant will refuse the message and nothing is sent or stored.

## Payment methods

We accept major credit and debit cards, and bank transfer for orders above 500. Cards are charged when an order ships, not when it is placed. An authorisation hold may appear on your statement when you place the order.

## Resetting your password

You can reset your password yourself. Choose "Forgot password" on the sign-in page and follow the link sent to your email address. The link expires after 30 minutes. Support cannot see or reset your password for you.

## Two-step sign-in

We recommend turning on two-step sign-in in your account settings. After that, signing in needs your password and a code from your authenticator app.

## Disputing a charge

If you see a charge you do not recognise, contact support with the order reference. We review charge disputes within 3 business days. While a dispute is open, no further charges are made for that order.
